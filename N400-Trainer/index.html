Your progress
Which civics test do you take?

USCIS uses the 2025 civics test if you filed your N-400 on or after October 20, 2025: the officer asks up to 20 questions and you pass with 12 correct. If you filed before that date you take the 2008 test: up to 10 questions, 6 correct to pass. Choose yours under My answers; the study cards and mock test follow it.

Both question lists follow the official USCIS lists (2025: form M-1778), with current officials as of October 2026. Answers about current officials can change after elections, so check uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates before your interview, and compare with the official lists at uscis.gov.

Speaking test

Practice the questions about your application

The officer will ask about your N-400 in English. Answer truthfully and the same way you answered on the form. If you don't understand a question, it is fine to say "Can you please repeat that?" or "What does that word mean?"

Hard words from the N-400

At the ceremony

Oath of Allegiance

I hereby declare, on oath, that I absolutely and entirely renounce and abjure all allegiance and fidelity to any foreign prince, potentate, state, or sovereignty, of whom or which I have heretofore been a subject or citizen; that I will support and defend the Constitution and laws of the United States of America against all enemies, foreign and domestic; that I will bear true faith and allegiance to the same; that I will bear arms on behalf of the United States when required by the law; that I will perform noncombatant service in the Armed Forces of the United States when required by the law; that I will perform work of national importance under civilian direction when required by the law; and that I take this obligation freely, without any mental reservation or purpose of evasion; so help me God.
